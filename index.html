My story

Hello, I'm John.

My name is Othusitse Saone John, and everyone knows me as John. SNUPA is my brand: a one-person business consultancy based in Palapye, Botswana. I do not run a big firm. I work for you personally, and every file that comes to me is handled by me from start to finish.

Starting a business in Botswana means dealing with CIPA, trade licensing, BURS and PPRA. For many first-time owners that paperwork is confusing and slow. I take it off your hands so you can concentrate on your customers.

Under the SNUPA brand I register companies and update their details, edit constitutions, handle BURS tax registration and clearance, apply for trading, industrial and manufacturing licences, register you for PPRA tenders, help you open a business bank account, and design logos and company profiles. You can reach me by WhatsApp or phone at any time to ask what you need and what it will cost.
