My story

Hello, I'm SNUPA.

My name is Othusitse Saone John, and I am a Motswana business consultant based in Palapye. I do not run a big firm. I work for you personally, and every file that comes to me is handled by me from start to finish.

Starting a business in Botswana means dealing with CIPA, trade licensing, BURS and PPADB. For many first-time owners that paperwork is confusing and slow. I take it off your hands so you can concentrate on your customers.

I register and restore companies, draft company constitutions, apply for trade licences, handle tax clearance and PAYE returns, and register PPADB codes. You can reach me by WhatsApp or phone at any time to ask what you need and what it will cost.

Company Registration

New private company registration with CIPA, from name reservation to certificate. Includes your bank opening letter and tax clearance certificate (TCC).

What to bring
  • Certified copy of Omang (or passport) for every director and shareholder
  • 2 or 3 company names you would like
  • Physical address and postal address of the business
  • Phone number and email address
  • What the business will do
  • Who owns how many shares
fromP1,500

Company Restoration

Restore a deregistered company to the CIPA register so it can trade again.

What to bring
  • Company name and registration number
  • Copy of the certificate of incorporation, if you still have it
  • Certified copy of Omang for each director
  • Contact details of the directors
fromP700

Company Constitution

Drafting of your company constitution to fit your shareholders and business.

What to bring
  • Company name and registration number
  • Names and certified Omang copies of all directors and shareholders
  • How many shares each person owns
  • Any special rules you want, for example how shares can be sold
fromP600

Trade Licence (SME)

Trade licence application for small and medium businesses.

What to bring
  • Certified copy of Omang for the owner or each director
  • Certificate of incorporation (for a company)
  • Proof of the business premises, such as a lease agreement or a letter from the landlord
  • Any special permit your type of business needs (I do not process these)
fromP600

Trade Licence (Commercial)

Trade licence application for larger commercial businesses.

What to bring
  • Certified copy of Omang for the owner or each director
  • Certificate of incorporation
  • Proof of the business premises, such as a lease agreement or a letter from the landlord
  • Details of the business activity
  • Any special permit your type of business needs (I do not process these)
fromP900

Tax Clearance Certificate

Registration and application for your BURS tax clearance certificate.

What to bring
  • Company name and registration number
  • Company tax number (TIN)
  • Certified copy of Omang for the directors
  • Proof that your tax returns are up to date
fromP200

PAYE Return

Preparation and submission of your PAYE return.

What to bring
  • List of your employees with their names and Omang numbers
  • Each employee's monthly salary
  • Your company tax number (TIN)
  • The month you are filing for
fromP150 / return

PPADB Code Registration

Registration of your PPADB codes for government tenders.

What to bring
  • Certificate of incorporation
  • Certified copy of Omang for the directors
  • Valid trade licence
  • Valid tax clearance certificate
  • Bank letter
  • Company profile and contact details
  • Qualifications or experience for the codes you want
fromP600

PPADB Additional Codes

Add further codes to an existing PPADB registration.

What to bring
  • Your current PPADB registration details
  • Valid trade licence and tax clearance certificate
  • Proof of qualifications, experience or equipment for the new codes
fromP400
