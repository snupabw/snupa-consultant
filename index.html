My story

Hello, I’m John.

My name is Othusitse Saone John, and everyone knows me as John. SNUPA is my brand: a one-person business consultancy based in Palapye, Botswana. I do not run a big firm. I work for you personally, and every file that comes to me is handled by me from start to finish.

Starting a business in Botswana means dealing with CIPA, trade licensing, BURS and PPRA. For many first-time owners that paperwork is confusing and slow. I take it off your hands so you can concentrate on your customers.

One person, one file, one phone number. You always know who is handling your paperwork.

Under the SNUPA brand I register companies and update their details, edit constitutions, handle BURS tax registration and clearance, apply for trading, industrial and manufacturing licences, register you for PPRA tenders, help you open a business bank account, and design logos and company profiles. You can reach me by WhatsApp or phone at any time to ask what you need and what it will cost.

Good to know

Before you start.

Do prices include government fees?

No. Prices shown are my fee. CIPA, BURS, PPRA and council fees are paid separately where they apply, and I tell you the exact amount before you pay anything.

Do I have to come to Palapye?

Most work is done remotely by WhatsApp, email and online filing. If a step genuinely needs you in person, I tell you before we start.

How long does it take?

It depends on the office handling your file. I confirm the realistic timeline for your case on WhatsApp before you pay, and I follow up until it is done.

What do you need from me?

Usually a certified copy of your Omang, company details if you have them, and proof of premises for a licence. Each service lists what to bring — open “What to bring” on the card.

Can you help me find tenders to bid for?

Yes. My tender board, DIGI TENDERS, lists open tenders from big companies in Botswana with closing dates and the official notice. I can also register you on PPRA and help you prepare the bid.
